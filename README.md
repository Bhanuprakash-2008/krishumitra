# KrishiMitra – Digital Assistant for Small Farmers

## 1. Project Overview
KrishiMitra is a digital assistant designed to bring essential farming support into one simple platform. It helps small farmers access plant identification, crop disease assistance, voice-based guidance, government schemes, expert support, agricultural products, and farming information.

## 2. Problem Statement
Small farmers may face difficulties identifying unfamiliar plants and crop diseases, understanding agricultural advice because of language barriers, finding reliable treatment guidance, accessing government schemes, and reaching agricultural experts. Information about weather, market prices, and farming support is often spread across different sources.

## 3. Proposed Solution
KrishiMitra provides a unified digital platform that combines AI-based plant-image analysis, multilingual assistance, voice guidance, government scheme discovery, farmer–expert community support, product recommendations, and planned weather and market information integrations.

## 4. Key Features
- **Photo-Based Plant Assistance:** Upload plant photographs to receive AI-generated predictions.
- **Voice-Based Guidance:** Help farmers understand agricultural information through spoken responses.
- **Government Schemes:** Discover relevant schemes and access application guidance.
- **Farmer and Expert Community:** Share experiences, ask questions, and seek expert advice.
- **Agricultural Product Recommendations:** Explore products relevant to farming needs.
- **Weather and Market Information:** Planned support for local weather and nearby market prices.
- **Multilingual Support:** Kannada, English, and Hindi are the target languages.

## 5. Technologies Used
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Plant Image Analysis: Pretrained PlantVillage-based image classification model
- Chat Assistance: FastAPI chat endpoint

## 6. Working Methodology
1. The farmer opens the platform.
2. The farmer uploads a plant photograph or requests agricultural guidance.
3. The backend processes the request using the relevant application functionality.
4. The system displays AI-generated predictions or available guidance.
5. The farmer can explore schemes, products, and other farming-support services.

## 7. Benefits
- Improves access to agricultural information.
- Makes guidance easier to understand through voice support.
- Brings multiple farming services together.
- Encourages knowledge sharing between farmers and experts.
- Helps farmers discover government support schemes and relevant products.

## 8. Future Scope
- Crop-specific disease detection models with offline access.
- Support for additional Indian languages.
- IVR phone-call or WhatsApp Business assistance.
- SMS and push notifications.
- Village-level market prices through FPO and mandi partnerships.
- Expert-verified advice and a government scheme eligibility checker.
- Integration with soil and weather sensors using ESP32.

## 9. Conclusion
KrishiMitra aims to make farming support more accessible, understandable, and convenient for small farmers by bringing AI-based plant assistance and essential agricultural services into one platform.
