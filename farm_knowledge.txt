Tomato needs watering every 2-3 days; check that the top 2 inches of soil are dry before watering.
Water at the base of the plant in the morning. Wet leaves at night help fungal diseases spread.
Early blight shows dark brown spots with yellow rings, starting on the lower leaves.
Late blight shows large water-soaked grey-green patches and spreads fast in cool, humid weather.
Remove and destroy badly infected leaves. Do not compost them.
Yellowing of older leaves can mean nitrogen deficiency or overwatering.
Leaf curl with yellowing and stunting can be caused by whitefly, which spreads leaf curl virus.
Yellow sticky traps help monitor and reduce whiteflies.
Neem oil spray can help control mild pest attacks such as aphids and whiteflies.
Rotate crops each season; do not plant tomato after tomato, potato or brinjal in the same field.
Use mulch to keep soil moisture even and reduce soil splash onto leaves.
Ask a Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture officer for pesticide names and doses.
